How it works

A single thread from first quote to final payment

Every step is recorded against the order — nothing lives in a chat history that disappears.

01

Request

A customer browses your catalog and requests a quotation with quantities and terms.

02

Quote

Your team adjusts pricing, discounts and shipping, then sends it back for approval.

03

Order

An accepted quote becomes an order and moves through your fulfilment stages.

04

Invoice

Invoices generate from the order and track partial or full payment automatically.

05

Paid

Payments settle through a connected provider, verified server-side, never guessed.

Security

Ownership that can't be quietly taken

One primary owner per organization, enforced where it counts — the database, not just the screen.

Server-side authorization

Roles and permissions are checked on every request, not hidden behind a frontend button.

Organization isolation

Every table is scoped to an organization. One company can never query another's rows.

Deliberate recovery

A forgotten password never spawns a new organization or a new owner — recovery keeps the same one.

Audit logging

Logins, role changes and owner-level settings changes are recorded and reviewable.

Verified payments

Payment status is confirmed by the provider's webhook — never taken on the frontend's word.

Scoped employee access

Each role — sales, finance, inventory, support — sees only its own corner of the workspace.

Questions

Before you get started

Can more than one person own the account?

No. Each organization has exactly one primary owner. Employees can be granted broad permissions, but ownership itself has to be deliberately transferred, never assumed.

What happens if the owner forgets their password?

They recover access through the standard reset flow and keep the same organization. Recovery never creates a new company or a new owner account.

Can our customers see each other?

No. Customer accounts are isolated from one another as strictly as separate organizations are.

What if we haven't connected a payment or AI provider yet?

Those features clearly show as not configured rather than faking a result. Everything works normally once you connect a real provider.
